I am a PhD student in Computer Science at UChicago, advised by Chenhao Tan.

Research Focus

  • Multimodal Large Language Model (LLM) Evaluation and Post-training for Healthcare
  • Human-AI Collaboration, specifically human-centered explanations and human-AI decision making

I am passionate about putting humans at the center of AI development, including how to understand the process of human-AI interaction and how we can better build/deploy AI tools that can actually be useful in real-world settings, especially in medical contexts.
