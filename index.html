Bio

I am a Master student in the College of Information Sciences and Technology at The Pennsylvania State University. I obtained my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I also visited Technische Universität München as an exchange student. Currently I am working with Professor Ting-Hao (Kenneth) Huang. We combine AI with crowdsourcing to facilitate better human-computer interaction.

Research Interests

I am always passionate in applying machine learning techniques to solve real-world problems. Moreover, I like to work with people. I believe the ultimate goal of AI is to benefit all of humanity, equally and broadly.

  • Applied Machine Learning (spatial-temporal data mining, healthcare)
  • Human Computer Interaction (combining AI with crowdsourcing)
  • Natural Language Processing (to support human-computer interaction)
  • Machine Learning (reinforcement learning, multimodal machine learning, Gaussian Process)
